# TRADE90: research quality and qualified audience growth

Goal: 10,000 daily users who engage with research. This is a target, not a traffic forecast or a promise of trading returns. Establish the current baseline before choosing a deadline or acquisition spend.

## This release
- Research overview with opposing contributions and inspectable limitations.
- Reported five-day accuracy versus majority baseline, sample and data freshness.
- Gold spot/futures separation; no synthetic conversion of futures levels into spot levels.
- Same-browser comparison between distinct research generations.
- Nine market-specific research entry pages linked from the terminal, with Gold, Bitcoin and USD/JPY linked from the homepage.
- Consent-aware GA events: research_market_selected and research_panel_opened. Values contain only instrument/panel names. Existing GA page measurement remains in place.

## Measure weekly
Use GA4 active users, engaged sessions, returning users and acquisition source by landing page. Review consented research events as an engagement sample, not a count of all visitors. Use Search Console query/page impressions, clicks, CTR, indexing and country reports. Filter internal and known bot traffic through the analytics platform. Do not count synthetic tests as audience growth.

Track milestones of 100, 500, 1,000, 3,000 and 10,000 daily active users using a 28-day trend. Do not assign dates before the current traffic baseline and channel capacity are known. Returning-user share and research engagement should accompany acquisition growth.

## First 30 days after publication
1. Inspect all nine research URLs in Search Console and confirm canonical/indexability/sitemap delivery. Check GA landing pages and the two research events with consent granted.
2. Prioritize the three pages with the strongest real impressions and engagement. Improve their specific explanations and links using observed queries; do not generate duplicate keyword pages.
3. Publish one carefully reviewed weekly explanation of a meaningful research change, with sources and timestamps, linking directly to the relevant market page. Draft newsletter/social distribution for owner review; no automatic sending authorized here.
4. Review errors, unavailable quotes and research age. Address provider reliability before increasing refresh frequency or promising real-time service.

## Data work requiring a separate validated dataset
- Obtain licensed, consistent historical spot gold data before migrating levels, charts and the model. Recompute features and rerun out-of-sample tests before calling the model spot-based.
- Verify five-day model performance using timestamped forecasts, non-overlapping outcomes, walk-forward splits and transaction-cost-aware evaluation before claiming a predictive edge.
- Validate calendar coverage; an empty event list must never be described as no event risk.
- Add server-side previous-research archives if comparisons must survive browser changes. Current comparisons are local to a browser and do not constitute a full forecast archive.

## Acquisition after evidence of retention
Develop source-backed educational research and useful calculator referrals with relevant educators. Measure each channel with campaign tags. Do not purchase fake traffic, mass-produce thin AI pages, send unsolicited promotions or buy links. Paid campaigns, partnerships and mailing require separate explicit authorization and a budget.

## Release limits
These are evidence-presentation and data-validation improvements. They do not retrain the upstream model, prove its forecasts, establish exact broker prices, or achieve the daily-user target by themselves. Search Console and GA reporting access are still needed to set an evidence-based growth schedule.
